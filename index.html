FAQ

Common questions, answered simply.

EcoTextile is a textile management platform designed to organize supported operational activities such as production records, materials, batches, inventory, scanning and reporting.
EcoTextile can be configured for textile businesses and teams that need a structured environment for their operational records and workflows.
Mobile access depends on the deployment and network configuration of the EcoTextile installation. When enabled, supported workflows can be accessed from compatible mobile devices.
The mobile scanner guide explains how to open the supported scanner workflow, allow camera access, scan the relevant code and review the resulting record.
First verify your login details and confirm that you are using the correct EcoTextile access address. If the issue continues, contact EcoTextile Support and provide the error message or a screenshot.
